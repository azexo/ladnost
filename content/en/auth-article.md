# Automation of Contract Processes in a Company

If you are reading this, it means your business has outgrown the stage where contracts are managed "in Excel and email." 
**Ladnost** is a CLM platform that transforms contract management from a zone of chaos and risks into a transparent, manageable asset.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation text-yellow-300 text-lg"></i> Symptoms You Cannot Ignore

Manual contract management at scale is direct financial loss. Are you familiar with these situations?

- Lawyers spend up to 70% of their time on routine tasks and version reconciliation, rather than risk assessment.
- The business is "blind": no one knows at what stage of approval a critical contract is.
- There is no single registry of obligations, leading to missed renewal deadlines and fines.
- Employees use outdated or unauthorized templates from the internet.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-chart-line text-green-300 text-lg"></i> How Business Changes with CLM Implementation

Automation is not just "another software for lawyers." It is a transition to predictable deal velocity.

## <i class="fa-solid fa-cogs text-blue-300 text-lg"></i> Solution Architecture in Ladnost

We have combined the best practices of corporate law and modern technologies to address these tasks:

- **Smart templating.** Generation of contracts from cards with dynamic variables.
- **Collaboration.** A single window for lawyers, business, and counterparties with a history of all edits.
- **Obligation tracking.** Centralized control of condition execution and automated date tracking.

> Automation does not replace lawyers. It frees up their time for strategic tasks 
> that truly bring value to the business, while the system takes on the routine.
