# Ladnost Privacy Policy

## 1. General Provisions
This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") defines the procedures for processing and protecting the personal data of users of the Ladnost platform.

**Personal data operator:** LLC "AZEKSO", TIN 5507273580.

## 2. Data Collected
We collect the following data:
- First name and last name
- Email address
- Password (in encrypted form)
- Information about documents and work within the system
- Technical data (IP address, device type, browser)

## 3. Purposes of Data Processing
Personal data is processed for the following purposes:
- Providing access to the Platform
- User identification
- Improving service quality
- Sending notifications and updates
- Compliance with legal requirements

## 4. Data Protection
We apply the following protection measures:
- Data encryption during transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Password hashing
- Restricted access to personal data
- Regular security audits

## 5. Data Storage
Personal data is stored on servers located within the territory of the Russian Federation, in accordance with the requirements of Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data."

## 6. User Rights
The user has the right to:
- Obtain information about their data
- Request correction, blocking, or deletion of data
- Withdraw consent to data processing
- File a complaint against the operator's actions with Roskomnadzor

## 7. Disclosure of Data to Third Parties
We do not disclose personal data to third parties, except in cases provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

## 8. Policy Changes
The Administration reserves the right to amend this Policy. Changes take effect from the moment of their publication.

## 9. Contact Information
For inquiries regarding personal data processing: info@ladnost.ru
